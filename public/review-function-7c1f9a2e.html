Function check

Does everything actually work?

This pass is only about behaviour — not how it looks. Walk the site the way a real member would and check that every step does what it should: forms submit, emails arrive, payment goes through, the right content unlocks, links go somewhere, nothing dead-ends. (A separate pass covers how it looks and feels.)

The charge is tiny, and refunded. Checkout is live, but the charge is just $0.50 (The Calm Library) or $1 (The Calm Circle) — pay with a real card or PayPal. When you’ve finished testing, just let us know and we’ll refund every charge. So please don’t hold back.

Each item has a quick Works / Found an issue toggle and a notes box. Be as blunt and technical as you like — a broken link, a flow that dead-ends, a wrong price or date, a console error, a redirect that lands in the wrong place. The more specific (which page, what you did, what happened), the faster it gets fixed.

Heads-up: the TAT for Animals library is filled in. The “Live Webinars” tab stays empty until Jez adds a webinar — there we’re only checking the shell and the access rules.

So we know whose feedback this is.

Covering everything without testing all day. You don’t need every possible combination — these four, between them, cover every payment type, billing period, and plan. Use them as your guide for the checkout steps below:

  1. Card · Monthly · The Calm Library
  2. PayPal · Yearly · The Calm Circle
  3. Card · Yearly · The Calm Library
  4. PayPal · Monthly · The Calm Circle

Then walk the journeys: upgrade (The Calm Library → The Calm Circle), cancel, password reset, and delete account.

Tip: use a fresh email per account and put the scenario right in it — e.g. hello+card-monthly-library@tatforanimals.com — so each result is easy to trace. (Anything at hello+…@tatforanimals.com lands in the same inbox but counts as a brand-new sign-up.)

A few things we handle on our side — no need to test these: the yearly renewal and its reminder email (they only happen much later). The “payment failed” state is also on us. And moving to a smaller plan, or changing a yearly plan, is done by us on request — so there’s no self-serve button for those on purpose (expected, not a bug).

1 · The core member journey

The path a new member takes. Start logged out, in a fresh browser if you can.

Sign up

Create an account and confirm the email

Do

From the home page, find the way to join and sign up with a real email you can open. Then confirm via the email we send.

Use a fresh email — not hello@tatforanimals.com, which is already registered (it would bounce to “already in use”). A personal address works, or hello+test@tatforanimals.com, which lands in the same inbox but counts as a brand-new sign-up.

Does the form validate and show clear errors? Does the confirmation email arrive, and does its link work and land in the right place? Try the “resend” path too.

Checkout

Choose a plan and pay by card

Do

Pick a plan and pay with your real card. The amount is tiny and refunded:

What you’ll be charged
$0.50 · The Calm Library
$1.00 · The Calm Circle

A real charge, but tiny — we refund every charge.

Heads-up: the plan pages still show the full $27 / $47 price, but the actual charge is the reduced amount above. Please use “Pay with card.”

Does it land on thank-you and then a dashboard that reflects the plan you bought, and does the welcome email arrive in your inbox? Try something tricky if you like: a logged-out checkout, or hitting pay twice — it should never double-charge or break.

Checkout · PayPal

Pay with PayPal — the other payment path

Do

Sign up again with another fresh email, pick a plan, and this time pay with PayPal instead of a card. PayPal is a separate payment system from the card checkout, so it needs its own check.

What you’ll be charged
$0.50 · The Calm Library
$1.00 · The Calm Circle

These amounts are already set on PayPal — a real charge, but tiny, and refunded.

Same checks as the card path: does it land on thank-you and a dashboard that reflects the plan you bought, does the account unlock the right content, and does the welcome email arrive?

Checkout · Yearly

Buy a yearly plan — the new annual option

Do

On the plans, flip the Monthly / Yearly toggle to Yearly, then sign up with a fresh email and pay — once with a card, and if you can, once with PayPal on another fresh email. Yearly is a separate plan from monthly, so it needs its own check.

What you’ll be charged
$0.50 · The Calm Library (per year)
$1.00 · The Calm Circle (per year)

The page shows the full yearly price ($270 / $470), but the actual charge is the tiny amount above — refunded.

After paying: does the dashboard show the yearly price and a next-renewal date about a year out, and does the welcome email arrive? (A yearly member should see no self-serve “upgrade” button — that’s expected.)

Account

The dashboard shows the right data

Do

On the dashboard, check the plan name, price, status, and next-renewal date are all correct for what you bought, and that the way to manage or cancel your membership is there and works.

Numbers and dates accurate? Anything mislabeled or missing? Do the buttons do what they say?

Library

The library + access rules

Do

Open the library. The TAT for Animals tab is filled in. Check the tabs/sections load and behave.

Then check who can see what: a Calm Library member can open the Calm Circle-only “Live Webinars” tab but should see a locked upgrade view — not the actual recordings; a Calm Circle member should see the real content. (That tab stays empty until Jez adds a webinar in the studio — her task below.) Flag anything that looks unguarded or errors.

Watch

Play a video — and pick up where you left off

Do

In the TAT for Animals library, open a video and let it play. Watch for at least half a minute, close it, then open the same one again.

Does it actually play? When you reopen it, does it resume near where you left off? Watch one to the end and check it gets a done mark. If any video won’t load or shows an error, note which one.

2 · Supporting flows

The flows a member hits less often, but that have to work.

Forgot & reset password

From sign-in, run “forgot password” → reset email → set a new password → sign in with it. Whole loop holds?

Sign in — password & magic link

Password sign-in works. The magic-link option (if shown) sends a link that signs you in. A wrong password shows a clear message.

Change password & sign out (from your account)

While signed in, open your account and use Change password — you should get an email with a link to set a new one (a different starting point from the logged-out “forgot password” above). Then use Sign out and confirm you’re actually logged out.

Manage / cancel subscription

How you cancel depends on how you joined. If you paid by card, click “Manage Subscription” — it opens a secure portal where you cancel (and can update your card there). If you paid by PayPal, there’s a “Cancel membership” button right on the dashboard, with a quick confirm step. Cancel on whichever you used: does it go through and confirm clearly, and does the cancellation email arrive? Re-subscribe after if you want to reset.

Upgrade: The Calm Library → The Calm Circle

As a monthly Calm Library member, find the upgrade to Calm Circle — on the dashboard or the Live Webinars card — and go through it. Does it move you to Calm Circle, charge only the price difference (never a double charge), and unlock the Calm Circle-only Live Webinars afterwards? (If you joined on Calm Circle, cancel and re-join on Calm Library to test this.) Note: yearly members don’t see a self-serve upgrade — those are handled by us — so test this on a monthly plan.

Delete account

From the account page (cancel any subscription first), request deletion. You should get a confirm step before anything happens, then an email with a link — try the “send again” option too. Does the whole loop complete correctly?

Webinar emails — Jez’s separate task (from the studio)

This one is on Jez and is separate from the member walk-through, because it only happens when content is published in the studio. Publish a test webinar (and upload a test recording), then check that a Calm Circle account you made receives the email about it. If nothing arrives, note it — the studio-to-email link needs a look.

3 · Across the board

Cross-cutting behaviour — things that don’t belong to one page.

Links, footer & legal pages

Every footer link, social icon, and the Privacy / Terms / Disclaimer pages load. No dead links, no 404s, no placeholder text left in.

On a real phone (not just resizing)

Best on a real phone, not just by shrinking a desktop window — that’s how most members will use it. Run the core journey (sign up → pay → dashboard → cancel) on your phone and watch for things that break: taps that miss, a checkout that won’t complete, the on-screen keyboard covering a field, a redirect that lands wrong. Are buttons and links big enough to tap? (How it looks is the separate design pass.)

A second browser

Try a different browser (Safari / Chrome / Firefox / Edge). Anything behave differently or error?
