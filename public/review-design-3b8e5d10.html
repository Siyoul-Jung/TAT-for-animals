Design check

Does it look and feel right?

This pass is only about the look and feel — not whether things work. Move through the site slowly and trust your eye: does each screen feel warm, calm and considered, does it hold together, does anything look off? (A separate pass covers whether everything works.)

What we were going for — a frame for your eye:

The site leans simple, warm and human — never slick or “AI-looking,” one clear purpose per screen, clean and uncluttered. If anything pulls against that — a screen that feels busy, cold, generic, or off-brand — that’s exactly what to flag.

Each item has a quick Looks right / Looks off toggle and a notes box. Trust small instincts — misalignment, uneven spacing, an odd colour, a font that looks wrong, an image that feels weak, a screen that feels cramped on a phone. The more specific (which page, which width, what felt off), the better.

To see the account screens (dashboard, library), you’ll need to sign up and check out — the charge is a tiny $0.50 / $1 on your real card and we refund it (details in section 2). The video library isn’t populated yet, so judge the shell, not the emptiness.

3 · Across the board

The visual things that don’t belong to one page.

On a phone

Go through it on a real phone. Does everything feel comfortable — text big enough, buttons easy to reach, nothing cramped, awkwardly spaced, or visually cut off? (Things that break go in the function pass; here it’s about how it looks and feels.)

Warmth & brand feel

Stepping back: does the whole thing feel like a real person’s warm, calm space — or does any part feel cold, generic, “AI-looking,” or cheap? Trust the gut read.

Consistency

Across pages: do type, colour, button styles, and spacing feel like one consistent hand? Anything that looks like it came from a different site?

4 · Anything else

Any visual hunch, a small detail that bugged you, an idea to make it warmer or cleaner. All of it helps.
